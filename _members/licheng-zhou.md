Licheng Zhou (周立成) is a master's student at the School of Data Science and Engineering, East China Normal University. He received his bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Donghua University in 2026.

His current research interests include performance optimization, the construction of performance-optimization agents, and the recursive self-improvement (RSI) of agents.

He completed two internships during his undergraduate studies: one at Cathay Capital ([cathaycapital.com](https://www.cathaycapital.com)) as an Agent Full-Stack Development Engineer, and one at ThinkingData ([thinkingdata.io](https://www.thinkingdata.io)) as an R&D Engineer on the Data Development Platform.
